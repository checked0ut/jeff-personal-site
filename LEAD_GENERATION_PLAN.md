# AI-Powered Lead Generation Service: Detailed Plan

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a detailed plan for offering AI-powered lead generation as a service, adapting the "Larry" research→generate→publish→track→iterate loop to B2B sales outreach.

---

## The Core Loop Applied to Lead Gen

| Step | Larry (Social) | Lead Gen Version |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Research | Scan competitors/trends | Find companies matching ICP, research decision makers |
| Generate | Create slideshow content | Craft personalized outreach messages |
| Publish | Post to TikTok/YouTube | Send via email/LinkedIn/Twitter |
| Track | Views + engagement | Replies, meetings booked, deals closed |
| Iterate | What's working → do more | Refine messaging, targeting, offer |
