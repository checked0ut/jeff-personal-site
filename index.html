I'm Jeff. I do product management at a B2B SaaS company by day, build small AI tools at night, and spend a lot of time thinking about why most PMs treat those as separate skill sets when they're really the same one. The skill is spotting a problem worth solving and shipping something against it.

Most PMs at my level have drifted from the business. The craft got better, the frameworks got prettier, the P&L got further away. I think that's backwards. A PM's job is to make the business money by making customers' lives better, and the PMs who'll matter over the next decade are the ones who can hold both ends of that sentence at once, use AI to move faster than the org around them, and ship instead of frame. This site is the evidence that I work that way.
