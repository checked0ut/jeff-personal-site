Board of Advisors

An AI agent I built in Gemini Enterprise to pressure-test product decisions against the perspectives of operators I respect.

The itch

Every product decision benefits from a second opinion, but the people whose second opinions I most want are not on my Slack.

What it is

A prompt framework that lets me ask a question and get answers from six different operators, each with their own lens. The six are Lenny Rachitsky, Tal Raviv, Greg Isenberg, Jeremy Clarkson, Adir Shiffman, and Adam Schwab. Two PM thinkers, two builder/operator types, and two Australian commercial operators whose instincts I trust. Jeremy Clarkson is in there because sometimes you need someone to tell you the answer is obviously stupid.

The real lesson

The output isn't a decision. It's a faster way to surface the angles I'd have missed. The PM still has to decide. The agent just makes sure the decision is informed.
