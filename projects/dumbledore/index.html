Dumbledore (OpenClaw)

A personal AI agent that runs the household admin I'd rather not.

The itch

Our son was born in late 2025. The volume of household decisions went up sharply, and the time available to make them went down sharply. I wanted to test whether a personal AI agent could pick up the parts of household admin that are repetitive, low-stakes, and easy to forget.

What's actually running, today

Solid Foods Planner. Every evening Dumbledore suggests what Joey should eat the next day, including how we're sequencing allergen introductions. On Fridays it tells us what's coming up the following week so the items go on the grocery list before the shop. This is the module that runs every day and gets used every day.

Calendar conflict scanner. Once a week it looks three weeks ahead at my calendar and my wife's calendar. When it finds an overlap where we both have something on, it flags it so we can figure out who's looking after our son before the conflict becomes a problem.

Other functionality exists at varying levels of maturity. The two above are the ones earning their keep right now.

What I learned

Two things most people would get wrong if they tried to build this.

  1. Starting too big. The temptation is to spec the whole household operating system on day one. The version that actually works started with a single module (solid foods) and grew from there.
  2. Underutilising what the agent can do. There's hype in both directions, people either expect magic or assume it can't do much. The reality is in the middle and only becomes visible by shipping a tiny thing, using it for two weeks, then deciding what to add.
